** Risk Register
*** Duplicate symbols when mixing SPM + CocoaPods for the same library
- Likelihood: Medium
- Mitigation: Migrate dependency clusters together (CocoaHTTPServer + its deps as a unit)

*** ObjC header visibility issues with SPM module maps
- Likelihood: Medium
- Mitigation: Test each migration thoroughly; may need custom ~module.modulemap~ files

*** MIKMIDI or SocketRocket break under SPM
- Likelihood: Medium
- Mitigation: Budget debugging time; consider vendoring source directly as fallback

*** Build config changes break Profile/Release/Test configurations
- Likelihood: Low
- Mitigation: Test all 4 build configurations after each phase
